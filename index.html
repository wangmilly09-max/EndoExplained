What is endometriosis?

Endometriosis is a long-term condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside it. It can cause inflammation, severe pain, and difficulty becoming pregnant. Experiences vary from person to person.

Pain that disrupts daily life deserves attention. Knowing the terms can help you describe your concerns to a healthcare professional.

Our purpose

EndoExplained makes research easier to approach for people living with endometriosis, their families, and anyone curious about the condition.

Each explainer identifies the original paper, describes how the research was done, and explains what questions the study leaves open.

How to read these summaries

Start with the takeaway, then look at how the researchers reached their conclusions. The original papers include full methods, funding disclosures, and any safety results. These educational summaries and have not been clinically reviewed.
